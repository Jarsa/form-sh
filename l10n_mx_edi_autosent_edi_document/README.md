Mexican Auto Sent Edi Document
==============================


This module allow us to send an email to the invoice customer once the
CFDI is signed, this message will contain as attachments the Invoice as
PDF and the CFDI (XML) and the minimum values of the invoice
(Customer Name, Invoice Name, Invoice Reference, Total amount,
Company and Sales  and salesperson).

============

- Not special pre-installation is required, just install as a regular Odoo
  module.
